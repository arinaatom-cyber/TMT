Anatomical coverage

120 projects · 38 broad organ/organ-structure classes · 69 detailed navigation categories

The 120 curated dataset packages cover 38 broad organ/organ-structure groups under an explicit aggregation scheme. These groups comprise 31 named-organ sites and seven organ/structure classes; they are not a count of individual organs, whole-organ specimens, or distinct anatomical subsites. Cervix is counted with uterus; caecum, colon, rectum and appendix with large intestine; jaw bones with bones; and diaphragm with skeletal muscles. The inventory was checked against selected source metadata, which resolve larynx, tongue, pharynx, lacrimal gland and spinal cord within broader navigation categories. The website and archive navigation retain 69 anatomical, tissue-source, model/reference and unresolved categories, with 227 project-category memberships. A project can occur in several categories and is counted once by exact Project ID. Measured-material classifications comprise 68 tissue projects, 35 cell-line projects, 15 primary-cell projects, and two projects combining cell lines and primary cells. Organ origin of a cell model does not establish that organ tissue was measured. The six model/reference navigation groups do not enumerate all individual cell lines. PXD012845 remains unresolved at the selected-column level; thymus is not added from unmatched project-level metadata.

All 38 classes have at least one tissue-project assignment. A tissue assignment may refer to a metastatic, surface or capsular specimen and does not establish sampling of normal organ parenchyma.

Verification and scope

All 240 archived Annotation/Samples files were reread and selected-sample joins reproduced for 120 exact Project IDs. This is not a new reading of all 93 source publications. Primary evidence for PXD027335 confirms primary astrocytes alongside cell lines; the selected matrix includes 21 cell-line profiles and three technical measurements of one primary-astrocyte biological sample. No quantitative values were changed.

Caecum in PXD030034 is retained in the source labels and included in the large-intestine rollup; it is not renamed colon. PXD012845 remains unresolved at selected-column level; thymus is not inferred from unmatched metadata.

Selected-source refinements: PDC000221 and PDC000519 use case-linked anatomical evidence; these labels do not assign every channel unambiguously to each organ. PDC000180 includes brain and spinal-cord sites; a skull location does not establish bone tissue. Lacrimal-gland evidence in IPX0004253000 does not establish whole-eye sampling.
